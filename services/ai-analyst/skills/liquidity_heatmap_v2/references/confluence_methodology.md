# SMC-Heatmap Confluence Methodology (v1.0)

To determine high-probability reversal zones, use the following scoring and confluence rules:

## 1. Confluence Strength (The "Gravity" Score)
| Confluence Type | Weight | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **H4 Order Block + Put/Call Wall (Density > 80%)** | Critical | Primary institutional entry/exit zone. |
| **FVG Gap + Max Pain alignment** | High | High statistical probability of gap fill towards the "Magnet". |
| **Gamma Wall + SMC "Change of Character" (CHoCH)** | Medium | Early signal of trend exhaustion/reversal. |

## 2. Rejection Reliability Table
| Density (%) | SMC Presence? | Reliability | Expected Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| > 90% | Yes (OB/FVG) | Very High | 80%+ Probability of immediate rejection. |
| 70 - 89% | Yes | High | Expect pinning behavior or slow reversal. |
| 70 - 89% | No | Medium | Liquidity exist but lacks structural "Hook". |
| < 50% | Any | Low | Likely to be sliced through unless news-driven. |

## 3. Decision Logic
- **LONG (Bullish)**: Price enters H1/H4 Demand Zone (OB) + Put Wall Density > 75%.
- **SHORT (Bearish)**: Price enters H1/H4 Supply Zone (OB) + Call Wall Density > 75%.
- **NEUTRAL**: Price in "Air" (No walls or OBs nearby) or conflicting signals (Wall at OB, but Trend is aggressive).
