# Deep Dive into Smart Money Concepts (SMC): The Hidden Logic of Price Action

This guide distills institutional trading logic specifically for the MTF Olympus system. It focuses on Liquidity as the primary fuel for price movement and how Market Makers (Algos) interact with retail resting orders.

## 1. Redefining Liquidity
- **Fuel for Price**: Price moves from one "Liquidity Pool" to another. 
- **Resting Orders**:
    - **Stop Loss (SL)**: Sell orders for Buys, Buy orders for Sells.
    - **Stop Orders**: Breakout triggers.
    - **Margin Calls**: Momentum accelerators.

## 2. Market Maker Behavioral Statistics
- **Fibonacci Trap**: Algorithms push price slightly beyond 61.8% / 78.6% levels to harvest SLs.
- **Psychological Numbers**: Deep liquidity clusters +/- 5-15 units around round numbers (e.g., .00, .50).
- **Water Well Analogy**: Liquidity sits below or above obvious support/resistance.

## 3. Liquidity Zones and Timing (The Critical Intersection)
- **Asian Range (00:00 – 08:00 UTC)**: Sideways movement building a range with liquidity on both sides.
- **London Open (08:00 – 10:00 UTC)**: Primary pivot point for the "Judas Swing" (Fakeout) before the real move.
- **Structural Landmarks**: 
    - **Previous Day High/Low (PDH/PDL)**: High density of overnight SLs.
    - **Equal Highs/Lows (EQH/EQL)**: Retail double tops/bottoms, identified as "Sitting Ducks."

## 4. The Liquidity Raid Strategy (Hunter Mode)
1. **Identify Obvious Levels**: Find support/resistance that is "too clean."
2. **Mark the Liquidity Zone**: Forecast SL locations (5-15 units away or 78.6% Fibo).
3. **Wait for the Raid**: Do NOT enter early. Observe the piercing and "sweep."
4. **Look for Rejection**: Fast V-Shape recovery or long-wick Pin Bar.
5. **Entry on Re-entry**: Enter only when price moves back into the previous range.
6. **Targeting**: Target the opposing Liquidity Pool.

## 5. Liquidity Void and Volume Gaps
- **Vacuum Effect**: Price moves extremely fast through zones where liquidity has been cleared.
- **Fair Value Gaps (FVG)**: Market imbalances to be filled.
- **Rule**: Never trade against a Void; follow the momentum.
